📖 Act 1: The Story of Aling Rosa's Workshop

Aling Rosa is a local barangay microentrepreneur who crafts handmade eco-bags and custom garments near local commercial centers. While her craftsmanship is high quality, she faces key challenges in scaling production durability and reaching online customers.


By implementing applied physics stress analysis in selecting fabric materials and integrating AI-assisted marketing tools, Aling Rosa transformed her small workshop into a modern, sustainable community microenterprise.

🔬 Applied Physics: Tensile Strength & Material Mechanics

To ensure business sustainability, products (bags and clothing) must resist mechanical failure under stress. Tensile Stress ($\sigma$) is calculated by dividing applied Force ($F$) by the material's Cross-Sectional Area ($A$):


$\text{Tensile Stress } (\sigma) = \frac{\text{Force } (F)}{\text{Area } (A)}$
